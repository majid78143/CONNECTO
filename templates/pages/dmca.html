Filing a DMCA Notice

If you believe content on CONNECTO infringes your copyright, send a notice to dmca@connecto.app including:

  • Your full name and contact information
  • Identification of the copyrighted work
  • URL(s) of the infringing content
  • A statement of good faith belief
  • Your electronic or physical signature

Counter-Notice

If you believe your content was wrongly removed, you may file a counter-notice. We will restore content after the statutory waiting period if no court action is filed.
